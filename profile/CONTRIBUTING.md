## 🧠 English

Welcome to **Vector-OS**! We’re an open-source community focused on building fast, clean, and modular systems.

### 📬 Contact Us to Contribute
- Open an issue titled: `[Join Request] Your Name`
- Include:
  - Your skills and interests
  - Why you want to join
  - Any previous experience

### 🚀 Submit Your Open-Source Project
- Share your idea or project by opening an issue or contacting the team.
- We will review and host it under the Vector-OS organization if accepted.

### ✅ Guidelines
- Write **clean and readable code**
- Share **original and unique** ideas
- Use **open-source licenses**
- Keep project folders organized (e.g. `src`, `docs`)
- Be respectful and collaborative
